# Phase 1 Iteration 2

## Routing Decision

Another behavioural iteration is required. The first slice improved the customer-facing ticket list, but the full slice has not yet converged because key customer-visible portal paths still stall or cannot be completed.

## Behavioural Gaps

- Customers can see safer status wording on the ticket list, but selecting a visible ticket does not take them into a usable detail view.
- Because ticket detail and status history are not reachable, customers cannot confirm the same status clearly across the main ticket-tracking surfaces.
- The help journey opens, but after the customer sends an initial message the experience does not visibly advance.
- Customers do not receive follow-up questions, a review step, a confirmation step, or another clear sign that the request journey is progressing.
- When downstream portal behaviour is unavailable or partial, the experience can appear stalled without a clear customer-facing explanation.

## Customer-Observable Problems

- A customer may believe ticket cards are non-functional because clicking a visible ticket does not open the next screen.
- A customer cannot independently verify ticket progress beyond the list view.
- A customer starting a new help request may believe the portal ignored their message because the screen remains visually static after send.
- A stalled intake flow increases the chance that support must restart the journey manually for the customer.

## Operational Intake Weaknesses

- Intake remains vulnerable to abandonment because the first submitted message does not lead to a visible next step.
- Support burden remains elevated because customers may need manual intervention to continue after an apparently successful message send.
- Ticket-tracking confidence remains limited because customers cannot move from list view into the deeper tracking surface.

## Scope Reminders

- Preserve the current improvement where customer-facing list statuses avoid internal support terminology.
- Keep this iteration focused on customer-visible status presentation and the immediate portal behaviours needed to make that presentation reachable and understandable.
- Do not broaden this slice into a wider portal redesign or unrelated support improvements.
- Keep internal workflows and operational handling unchanged.

## Behavioural Priorities

- Highest priority: customers must be able to open a usable ticket detail/history view from the visible ticket list.
- Highest priority: the help journey must visibly progress after the first customer message.
- High priority: customers should receive a clear, understandable response when the portal cannot continue a live assistant flow.
- High priority: customer-facing status wording should remain aligned across every reachable ticket-tracking surface in this slice.
