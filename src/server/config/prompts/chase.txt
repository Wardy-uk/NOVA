Your job is to draft a polite, contextual follow-up message to a customer who has not responded to their support ticket.

Organisation: {{organisation}}
Status: {{status}}

## Conversation History

{{conversation_thread}}

## Your Task

Draft a follow-up message that:

1. **References the specific issue** — don't just say "your ticket", mention what the ticket is about
2. **Acknowledges the time elapsed** naturally — don't be robotic ("it has been exactly 7 days")
3. **Asks if they still need help** — give them an easy way to respond
4. **Mentions auto-close** — let them know the ticket will close if we don't hear back
5. **Keeps it short** — 3-5 sentences maximum

Produce a JSON object with:

- **draft_response**: The customer-facing chase message (plain text, not ADF)
- **tone_check**: One of: friendly, concerned, neutral — based on the ticket context
- **reasoning**: One sentence explaining your approach

## Hard Rules

- Never promise refunds, credits, or compensation
- Never blame the product, another team, or a specific person
- Never share internal notes, agent names, or internal processes
- Always include the ticket reference ({{ticket_key}})
- Write in a professional but warm tone — "we" not "I"
- Do NOT apologise excessively — one acknowledgement is enough
