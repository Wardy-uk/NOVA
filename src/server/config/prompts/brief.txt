## Your Task

Generate a structured brief that helps the agent resolve this ticket quickly. Include:

1. **customer_summary**: 2-3 sentences about who this customer is, their tier, and recent interaction history
2. **ticket_analysis**: What the issue is, what's been tried, what the current state is
3. **recommended_approach**: Specific, actionable steps the agent should take. Reference KB articles or similar tickets if relevant. Be direct — "Check if X, then do Y" not "Consider investigating"
4. **kb_references**: Array of relevant KB article titles/IDs
5. **similar_tickets**: Array of similar resolved tickets with key, summary, and how they were resolved
6. **estimated_complexity**: "simple" (known fix, KB match), "moderate" (investigation needed), or "complex" (multi-system, escalation likely)
7. **key_risks**: What could go wrong or make this harder than it looks
8. **suggested_skills**: What skills/knowledge the ideal agent would have for this ticket

## Rules
- Be specific and actionable, not vague
- Reference actual ticket keys and KB articles where possible
- If you don't have enough information, say so rather than guessing
- Never suggest promising timelines, refunds, or compensation
- Focus on what will help the agent resolve this fastest
